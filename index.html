The trust gap

AI use is normal. Disclosure still feels awkward.

The badge gives teams a lightweight signal that travels with the work.

Marketing teams use AI to draft campaign briefs, polish stakeholder emails, generate social copy, and speed up deck creation. Without a shared signal, recipients are left guessing how much AI was involved and what level of review is needed.

"Did AI write this campaign brief? Was this researched or generated? Can I trust these insights? Should I verify this before presenting to leadership?"

The solution

A small badge with a clear job.

Use one badge per artifact and choose the highest level of AI involvement that occurred.

One Badge, Clear Signal

Pick a badge that matches your workflow. Drop it in your email footer, slide deck, or document header.

Process, Not Ownership

Decision guide

Choose the right badge in one pass.

Start with the highest level of AI involvement. When unsure, use Human–AI Co-Created.

No AI wrote content

Use Human Original, Human Original • AI Polished, or Human Written • AI Reviewed.

AI helped generate options

Use AI Suggested • Human Approved or Human Curated when a human selected and shaped the result.

AI wrote meaningful text

Use Human–AI Co-Created, AI Drafted • Human Edited, AI Drafted, or AI Generated.

How to add to Outlook

Use the PNG footer for the best compatibility with Windows desktop and corporate email clients.

  1. Choose “Email footer (PNG, safest)”.
  2. Click “Copy code”.
  3. Open Outlook: File, Options, Mail, Signatures.
  4. Paste the footer into your signature.
  5. If HTML is blocked, use “Copy PNG” and insert the image manually.
  6. Send yourself a test email to confirm rendering and alt text.
